Live incidents and service updates

If you already have production access, you can receive service updates and raise live incidents affecting an API you consume.

To raise a live incident, contact the HMCTS Digital and Technology Solutions service desk. Do not contact them before you have completed onboarding and been issued production credentials.

Give us feedback

This service is in beta and we are actively improving it. You can give feedback at any time, including suggesting APIs or capabilities you would like to see added.

If you would like to help us user-test the marketplace as we develop it, get in touch and say so — that is more useful to us than almost anything else.
